A segmented run has two pages: the switch at the top

A segmented run computes the unsegmented branch in full (it is half of the zone's answer), so step 9 writes it as its own page too: report_unsegmented.html, next to report.html. Both open with a switch, Segmented and Unsegmented only, that goes from one to the other. The unsegmented page is the report an unsegmented run of the zone gives: the same branches, curve and band, against PTHA18's unsegmented branch, with no LEVEL 0 section. Nothing is run twice; the engine only adds the branch's own percentile band (exceedance_rate_percentiles_unsegmented.csv), because the segmented run's band is the zone mix. On kermadectonga2 and kurilsjapan the page differs from <zone>_v9/report.html only in the switch, the box under the title (green, "UNSEGMENTED VIEW OF A SEGMENTED RUN") and the folder name; validate_v9.py unseg_view checks the numbers under it bit for bit. An unsegmented run has one page and no switch.

The box under the title says which kind of run it is

Right under the header, a green box says UNSEGMENTED RUN (every number on the page is the zone's), or an amber box says SEGMENTED RUN (LEVEL 0), lists the segments with their columns and where the boundaries came from, and repeats which blocks describe the unsegmented branch and which the whole zone.

Union of segments or source zone?The union of segments is the segments added up: the zone seen as several faults that each break on their own (exceedance_rate_union_of_segments). The source zone is the zone's actual answer, half the zone as one fault and half the union: 0.5 x unsegmented + 0.5 x union (exceedance_rate_source_zone). On kermadectonga2_v9seg at Mw 7.2: unsegmented 0.2251, union 0.2790, source zone 0.2521 per year. A hazard study uses the source zone; the union is one of its two halves.
Does prior vs posterior depend on segmenting?No, for the figure on the page: it is the unsegmented branch, and segmenting does not change that branch at all (same area, coupling, earthquakes, weights; its curve is identical in kermadectonga2_v9 and kermadectonga2_v9seg). But each segment has its own update, against the earthquakes on its own cells (tonga 3, kermadec 6, hikurangi 0), with its own TVD in logic_tree_summary.csv and its own figure fig_branch_fan_<zone>_<segment>.png. A segment with no earthquakes is updated too: seeing none in 41 years rules out the branches that predict many (kermadectonga2_v9seg TVD: zone 0.69, tonga 0.68, kermadec 0.51, hikurangi 0.57).

Why the polygons are re-indexed before drawingThe two official files do not store their cells in the same order: the netCDF statistics table is down-dip major ((1,1), (2,1), (3,1), (4,1), (1,2)…) while the shapefile is along-strike major ((1,1), (1,2), (1,3)…). Taking the shapefile's rows in file order would pair every polygon with another cell's depth and convergence: wrong, but plausible-looking. The polygons are therefore indexed by (downdip_number, alongstrike_number) and read out in the netCDF's own order. Checked on kurilsjapan, kermadectonga2 and puysegur2: cell centroids equal to the table's to 0.0000°.
